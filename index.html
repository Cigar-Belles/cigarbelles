The Founders

Two Belles, one table.

Kira Sholeen

Co-founder · York, PA

Shirah M. Brown

Co-founder · Philadelphia

We met over a cigar at Ashton's and recognized each other right away: two women with similar paths, a love of a fine smoke, and a habit of introducing everyone we meet to someone they should know.

Kira in York, Shirah in Philadelphia, we kept finding the same thing. The best conversations happen in the lounge, and too few women are in the room. Philly Cigar Belles is our answer: a table where women connect, light up, and leave knowing people worth knowing.
